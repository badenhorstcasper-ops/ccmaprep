⚠ Why this matters now
  • Labour inspectors can arrive unannounced.
  • A compliance order can be issued on the first visit. There may be no warning and no second chance.
  • Under section 76A of the BCEA, an underpayment fine can be up to twice the amount underpaid or twice the employee's monthly wage, whichever is more, for a first offence. Repeat offences cost more.

Often it is a simple mistake, like 4 or 5 workers out of 20 on the wrong wage rate. A tidy file helps you find and fix that first.
